ReleaseProof privacy policy

Effective 31 October 2026 · updated 5 November 2026 · Nightjar Labs

What the app stores

ReleaseProof is a Jira issue panel built on Atlassian Forge. For each Jira issue where it is used, it stores:

  • the release policy you enter (identifiers, thresholds, slice names);
  • the prediction CSV you import (row IDs, slice labels and numeric values);
  • approval records: the Atlassian account ID of the approver, the time, and the fingerprint they approved.

Do not import personal or sensitive data in prediction files. The app is meant for model-evaluation numbers.

Where it is stored

All data is kept in Forge storage on Atlassian's platform, scoped to your site installation and keyed by Jira issue. The app requests only the read:jira-work, read:jira-user and storage:app permissions. The read:jira-user permission is used only to look up the approver's display name from Jira when the panel is shown (or evidence is exported); the name is never stored by the app. It makes no requests outside Atlassian, uses no analytics or third-party services, and Nightjar Labs does not copy app data to its own systems.

Access and deletion

Anyone who can view the Jira issue can view its release state; editing and approval require Jira edit permission on that issue. Uninstalling the app removes access to its data; Atlassian's Forge data-retention rules apply to stored data after uninstall.
